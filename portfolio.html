Your details
Use a clear, friendly photo, just like on LinkedIn. Your headline should say exactly the job you want.
Mention your time zone. Remote employers care about it.
About me
2–4 sentences: who you help, what you do, and one result or strength. Skip "hardworking and God-fearing". Show it instead.
Skills & tools
Copy keywords from the job posts you're applying to. Separate each one with a comma.
Work samples & projects
No experience yet? Do sample projects and call them "sample". That's honest and it still counts. Add a link to each one (Google Drive, Canva or Notion, set to "anyone with the link").
Testimonial
Help one small business for two weeks, then ask them for 2–3 sentences about working with you. Leave this empty until you have a real one.
Certificates & training
Free certificates count. HubSpot Academy has free ones for marketing, sales and customer service. One per line.
Contact
Use a professional email, like firstname.lastname@gmail.com.

Your edits are saved on this device automatically.

On your phone, tap Download, then choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.

Got your PDF? Here's what to do next.

  1. Upload it to Google Drive and set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view".
  2. Put that link everywhere: your CV, LinkedIn "Featured" section, and every job application.
  3. Replace sample projects with real ones as you get them, then download a fresh PDF.
  4. Apply consistently. Five to ten good, tailored applications a day beats 100 in one night.
